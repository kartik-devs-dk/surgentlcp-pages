Physician-level critique of opposing life care plans for defense counsel and insurers — testing every recommendation against the medical evidence.

The challenge only a physician can make

Most rebuttal experts can question an opposing plan's arithmetic — pricing sources, duplications, inflation assumptions. Useful, but it leaves the plan's foundation untouched: the medical recommendations themselves.

Challenging those recommendations is a medical opinion, and scope of practice reserves it to physicians. A nurse rebuttal expert cannot testify that a projected revision surgery is not medically indicated. A board-certified surgeon can — item by item, from the operating-room experience the jury can weigh.

That is the difference between trimming an inflated plan and dismantling it.

What our rebuttal analysis examines

Medical necessity — are the recommended treatments supported by the records and clinical standards, or are they speculative?

Frequency and duration — do therapy schedules, follow-up intervals, and lifetime durations match clinical norms?

Duplication — items counted twice across categories, overlapping services, equipment replaced on implausible cycles.

Costing — chargemaster-level prices where documented lower costs apply; unsourced or outdated figures.

Causal relation — items attributable to pre-existing conditions or unrelated to the injury at issue.

Frequently asked questions

What is a life care plan rebuttal?

An expert analysis of an opposing life care plan that tests each element against the medical evidence — necessity, frequency, duplication, costing, and causal relation — delivered as a written critique and, where engaged, testimony.

How long does a rebuttal take?

Typically two to four weeks — faster than an original plan, since the scope is defined by the document under review. We work to expert-disclosure and deposition calendars; share your dates and we'll confirm feasibility first.

Do you only work for the defense?

Rebuttal engagements are typically defense-side, but the same clinical objectivity runs both directions — we prepare original plans for plaintiff counsel and critiques for defense counsel. The medical evidence decides every opinion, which is what makes them credible on either side.

Can the rebuttal expert testify?

Yes. Every rebuttal is authored by a board-certified surgeon prepared to defend it at deposition and trial — that prospect alone often changes settlement posture.
