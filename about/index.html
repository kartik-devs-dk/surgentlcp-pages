About

Built on a different standard.

Surgent LCP was founded on the principle that a surgeon who is also a Certified Life Care Planner produces more credible, more defensible medical opinions.

What sets us apart

The life care planning field is served primarily by rehabilitation professionals and nurses. Surgent LCP was built on a different foundation — board-certified surgeons who bring direct clinical expertise to every engagement.

That surgical credential is what distinguishes every opinion we produce.

We deliver faster, more credible, and more rigorously documented opinions — across a full range of medical expert services.
