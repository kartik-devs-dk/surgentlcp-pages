About

Built on a different standard.

Surgent LCP was founded on the principle that a surgeon who is also a Certified Life Care Planner produces more credible, more defensible medical opinions.

What sets us apart

The life care planning field is served primarily by rehabilitation professionals and nurses. Surgent LCP was built on a different foundation — board-certified surgeons who bring direct clinical expertise to every engagement.

That surgical credential is what distinguishes every opinion we produce.

We deliver faster, more credible, and more rigorously documented opinions — across a full range of medical expert services.

Our services

Life Care Plans

Comprehensive, surgeon-authored life care plans for catastrophic injury, malpractice, and complex litigation.

Medical Cost Projections

Surgeon-signed projections of future medical costs — designed for pre-litigation, demand letters, and early resolution.

Independent Medical Examinations

Objective, board-certified surgical examination and review — covering medical records, treatment plans, and causation opinions.

Reasonable Billing Analysis

Surgeon-authored analysis of billed medical charges — assessing reasonableness, necessity, and relatedness.

Causation Opinions

Expert surgical opinions on mechanism of injury, causation, and the relationship between incident and diagnosis.

Medical Record Review & Summary

Concise, clinically accurate summaries of complex medical records — reviewed through a surgeon's lens.

Expert Consultation

Direct access to a board-certified surgeon for consultation on complex medical questions, case strategy, or record review.
